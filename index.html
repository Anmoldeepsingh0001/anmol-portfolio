About

Close to five years in front of the camera, across boardrooms, coastlines, and everything between.

I'm a model, actor, and content creator based in Prince Edward Island, building lifestyle and finance content as @anmol.finance. My work moves easily between tailored business editorials, sunlit coastal campaigns, and hospitality film — shot regularly alongside filmmakers Simon Reid Studios and Brady McCloskey.

Over the years I've worked alongside numerous models across the industry, and I'm genuinely passionate about being in front of the camera — comfortable on set, and always at ease however the light or the brief changes. Beyond modelling, I bring real video experience to the table — editing, videography, and on-camera acting — so I'm just as comfortable carrying a scene as I am a look.
